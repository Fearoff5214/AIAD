# India GST Compliance Knowledge Pack

## E-invoice Core Compliance
- E-invoice is generated through the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) for notified taxpayers.
- GSTIN of supplier and recipient, invoice number, invoice date, taxable value, tax amounts, and HSN details are critical.
- Invoice Reference Number (IRN) and QR code are generated by IRP for valid payload.
- Business should validate master data quality (GSTIN, place of supply, item taxability) before submission.

## E-way Bill Core Compliance
- E-way bill is required for movement of goods above applicable threshold as per prevailing rules.
- Key fields: supplier details, recipient details, item details, transporter details, distance, and mode.
- Part A captures transaction details; Part B captures vehicle and transporter movement details.
- Keep cancellation and update windows in mind as per current GST/transport rules.

## Manufacturing Compliance Checklist
- Maintain item-wise HSN mapping and GST rate validation.
- Ensure BOM outputs and stock movements match invoice and e-way bill values.
- Validate job work, branch transfer, and delivery challan scenarios correctly.
- Track e-invoice and e-way bill status for each outbound invoice and delivery note.

## Wholesale Compliance Checklist
- Maintain customer GST category and place-of-supply accuracy.
- Validate discount/tax treatment consistency across bulk invoices.
- Reconcile outward supplies with returns preparation data monthly.
- Monitor cancellation, credit notes, and debit notes with proper reference documents.

## Returns and Reconciliation Practices
- Reconcile sales register, IRN logs, and e-way bill logs periodically.
- Investigate failed API submissions quickly and correct master/transaction data.
- Maintain audit trail for corrections, cancellation reasons, and amended documents.
- Review latest GST notifications/circulars and align system validations.

## Safe Answering Boundaries
- Thresholds, effective dates, and legal references can change.
- User should verify final compliance position with latest CBIC notifications and a qualified CA.
