# India GST Compliance Knowledge Pack

## GST Foundations
- GST is a destination-based indirect tax with CGST, SGST/UTGST, and IGST components.
- Intra-state supply typically applies CGST + SGST/UTGST, while inter-state supply applies IGST.
- Taxability depends on nature of supply, place of supply, GST registration status, and product/service classification.
- HSN/SAC based classification and correct tax category are foundational for valid invoices and return reporting.

## GST Rate Structure and Classification
- Common rate buckets include nil/exempt, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%, but product-specific notifications control applicability.
- GST rates must be tied to HSN/SAC classification and current notifications, not free-text assumptions.
- Non-GST and exempt items should be clearly identified and should not be mixed with taxable logic incorrectly.
- Maintain versioned tax mapping for HSN/SAC changes and effective dates.

## Place of Supply and Tax Type
- Place of supply determines whether a transaction is in-state or out-state.
- Correct state code validation for supplier, recipient, and place of supply is required for tax type accuracy.
- Incorrect place of supply can cause IGST vs CGST/SGST mismatch and return reconciliation issues.
- SEZ, overseas, and special transaction categories need explicit handling.

## E-invoice Core Compliance
- E-invoice is generated through the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) for notified taxpayers.
- Critical fields: supplier GSTIN, recipient GSTIN (as applicable), invoice number/date, line-item HSN, taxable value, and tax breakup.
- IRN and signed QR code are generated by IRP for valid payloads.
- Validate masters before submission: party GST category, HSN/SAC, item tax template, and place of supply.

## E-way Bill Core Compliance
- E-way bill applies for movement of goods based on prevailing statutory conditions.
- Part A captures transaction details and Part B captures transporter/vehicle movement details.
- Goods/service distinction, transporter details, distance, and document linkage are critical.
- Cancellation/update windows should be handled according to current rules.

## Credit Note and Debit Note Controls
- Credit/debit notes must reference the original tax document and preserve traceability.
- Tax impact should follow original supply context and applicable adjustments.
- Reversal and amendment entries must be reflected consistently in returns and ledgers.

## Reverse Charge and Special Cases
- Reverse charge scenarios require separate tax accounting treatment and validation.
- Export/SEZ and overseas categories require category-specific checks and document controls.
- Ensure legal category selection is consistent with GSTIN patterns and address metadata.

## Returns and Reconciliation Practices
- Reconcile books with outward supplies, inward supplies, e-invoice logs, and e-way bill logs.
- Investigate failed API submissions quickly and correct master/transaction data.
- Keep an auditable trail for corrections, cancellation reasons, and amended documents.
- Run periodic mismatch checks for GSTIN, taxable value, tax rate, and document references.

## Manufacturing Compliance Checklist
- Maintain item-wise HSN mapping and GST rate validation.
- Ensure BOM outputs and stock movements match invoice and transport documents.
- Validate job work, branch transfer, and delivery challan scenarios.
- Track e-invoice/e-way bill status for each outbound invoice and delivery note.

## Wholesale Compliance Checklist
- Maintain customer GST category and place-of-supply accuracy.
- Validate discount/tax treatment consistency across high-volume invoices.
- Reconcile outward supplies against filing-ready return datasets monthly.
- Monitor cancellation, credit notes, and debit notes with strict document linkage.

## ERP and Data Governance
- Keep Item master, GST HSN Code master, and Item Tax Template data synchronized.
- Avoid manual override of GST rates unless approved and logged.
- Use policy-driven tax templates and auto-assignment from HSN/SAC.
- Track policy effective dates and update mappings via controlled release process.

## Copilot Response Structure
- Explain: why the rule applies.
- Check: what fields to verify in ERP data.
- Action: practical next steps for compliance-safe processing.
- Caution: advise user to verify dynamic legal thresholds and latest notifications.

## Safe Answering Boundaries
- Thresholds, effective dates, and legal references can change.
- If context is insufficient, answer with uncertainty and request verification.
- Final compliance decisions should be verified with latest CBIC notifications and a qualified CA.
