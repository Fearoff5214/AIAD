❌ Error 1 — Wrong GST Rate (Row 1: Industrial Motor)
Industrial Motors (HSN 8501) attract 18% GST under Schedule IV of GST tariff. Applied rate is 12%, causing ₹3,600 under-charge. Correct tax = ₹10,800 not ₹7,200. This creates a liability mismatch in GSTR-1 filing.
❌ Error 2 — GST Charged on Exempt Item (Row 2: Fresh Vegetables)
Raw/unprocessed fresh vegetables (HSN 0701) are fully exempt from GST (0% rated). Charging 5% GST of ₹250 is illegal and must be reversed. Supplying exempt goods with GST violates Section 8 of the CGST Act and may attract penalties.
❌ Error 3 — Tax Amount Mismatch (Row 3: Steel Bolts)
Taxable value ₹6,000 × 18% = ₹1,080 (correct). Invoice shows ₹1,500 — an over-charge of ₹420. The recipient cannot claim full ITC on the over-charged amount. This will cause GSTR-2A mismatch and ITC reconciliation failures.
